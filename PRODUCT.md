# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Primary user is Ira (wife of the author), opening a digital birthday surprise from her husband.

## Product Purpose
A romantic, interactive birthday countdown and greeting card for Ira's 21st birthday (September 19, 2026). The goal is to make her feel deeply loved and appreciated through a premium, deeply personal digital experience.

## Positioning
A digital "memory box" / secret surprise that unlocks at midnight on her 21st birthday, blending personal letter, photo gallery, and interactive wishes.

## Operating Context
Viewed on mobile or desktop web browser, likely in an intimate setting. Includes a musical background (music.mp3) that sets a romantic mood.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must include a countdown timer locked until Sept 19, 2026.
- Must support a "husband mode" secret password ("1909", "ирочка", "21") for early access.
- Non-AI aesthetic: Avoid the "AI-generated look" (cream ground, burgundy serif, generic layouts). Needs high-end, premium craft.
- Interactive elements: Background music toggle, unlock mechanism.

## Brand Commitments
- Tone: Highly romantic, sincere, premium.
- Existing assets: `music.mp3`, specific dates/passwords.

## Evidence on Hand
- Placeholder images (Unsplash) in the gallery section (to be replaced by the husband later).
- Personal text (birthday letter).

## Product Principles
1. Impeccable Craft: The design must feel handcrafted, expensive, and meticulously detailed, far beyond generic templates.
2. Emotional Resonance: Every interaction (unlocking, reading, playing music) should enhance the romantic feeling.
3. Focused Anticipation: The locked screen timer should build genuine anticipation without feeling like a mere utility.
